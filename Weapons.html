Weapons

About Weapons

In Soul Knight, weapons are essential tools for survival in the dungeons. There are various types of weapons, each with unique characteristics and abilities.

Weapon Categories

Melee Weapons

Close-range weapons that deal damage in direct contact with enemies. These include swords, axes, and hammers.

Ranged Weapons

Long-range weapons that fire projectiles. These include pistols, rifles, shotguns, and bows.

Magic Weapons

Special weapons that use energy to create magical effects. These include staffs and wands.

Special Weapons

Unique weapons with special abilities and effects. These weapons often have unusual firing patterns or special mechanics.

Weapon Rarity

Common (White) - Basic weapons with standard stats

Uncommon (Green) - Improved weapons with better performance

Rare (Blue) - High-quality weapons with enhanced abilities

Epic (Purple) - Powerful weapons with unique effects

Legendary (Orange) - The most powerful weapons in the game
